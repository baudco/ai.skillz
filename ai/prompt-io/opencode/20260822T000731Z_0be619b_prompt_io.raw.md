workflow after duplicate implementation was performed without first
checking branches, worktrees, topic history, and affected-path history.

The `git-mgmt` skill is expanded to trigger before substantial branch-bound
work and commits. It requires literal-safe topic/history searches, inventories
dirty paths in every registered worktree, excludes the active implementation
baseline, and avoids candidate-base guesses. It reports reusable work without
suggesting rebase/restack and adds a commit-time backstop. `open-wkt` and
both `commit-msg` and `commit-plan` explicitly invoke the gate, with
manifest-enforced dependencies.
